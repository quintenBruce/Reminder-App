Remind for Android

The web app has retired. Your reminders remain in your Google account.

  1. Ask your friend for the Remind APK and install it on your Android phone.
  2. Open Remind, sign in with Google, and choose your name and username.
  3. Return to your invitation message and tap the link again. Review the name, then tap Add Friend.

If the link opens here again, open Android Settings → Apps → Remind → Open by default and enable supported links.

No iPhone or browser app is available.
